Privacy Policy隐私政策

Last updated: November 21, 2026

The short version: OpenWire collects no personal data. There's no account, no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking, and we run no servers. Your device reads public news sources directly and summarizes them on the device. Your saved stories, reading history and settings stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, sync through your own private iCloud account — which we cannot access.

简要说明：开讯不收集任何个人数据。无需注册、无广告、无第三方统计或追踪，我们也没有服务器。新闻由你的设备直接从公开来源读取并在本机生成摘要；收藏、阅读记录和设置只保存在你的设备上，开启 iCloud 时仅通过你自己的私有 iCloud 同步，我们无法访问。

1. Information We Collect 我们收集的信息

None. We don't ask for an account and don't collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier or any other personal information. We have no server that could receive it.

2. How Stories Are Loaded 内容如何加载

To show you news, your device connects directly to public websites and feeds — for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Hacker News (via the Algolia API), InfoQ, arXiv, Hugging Face, DEV Community, GitHub, TechCrunch and the other sources listed in the app, plus any RSS sources you add yourself. As with any web request, those sites receive standard technical information such as your IP address and the app's user agent, under their own privacy policies. No information about you is added to these requests.

Summaries, key points and topic labels are produced by an algorithm that runs entirely on your device. Article text is never sent to us or to any AI service.

When you open an original article, it loads in an in-app Safari view, where the publisher's site and its own privacy policy apply.

iCloud sync uses Apple's CloudKit private database and iCloud key-value storage in your own Apple Account. Only you can access that data; we can't see it. If you're not signed in to iCloud, everything stays on the device.

4. Translation 翻译

Chinese and other languages are produced with Apple's on-device Translation framework. Apple may download language models to your device; the text you read is translated locally.

5. Notifications & Background Refresh 通知与后台刷新

The daily briefing reminder and keyword alerts are local notifications created on your device — we don't operate a push server. With Background App Refresh on, iOS may occasionally wake the app to check your sources for new stories; this happens on your device.

6. In-App Purchase 应用内购买

OpenWire Pro is an optional one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details; the app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether Pro has been purchased. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Data Sharing 数据共享

We don't have your data, so we never sell, rent or share it.

8. Children's Privacy 儿童隐私

OpenWire is a news app for professionals and isn't directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Your Choices & Deleting Your Data 你的选择与数据删除

  • Clear the story cache: Settings › Sources › Clear cache & re-fetch.
  • Remove a saved story: swipe it left in Settings › Library › Saved, or tap the bookmark again.
  • Remove your sources or keywords: swipe to delete in Settings › Pro.
  • iCloud data: iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › OpenWire.
  • Uninstall: deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

10. Changes to This Policy 政策变更

If the way the app handles data changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
