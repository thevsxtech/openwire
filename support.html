Frequently Asked Questions常见问题

How are the summaries made?摘要是怎么生成的？

On your iPhone. OpenWire reads the original article and an algorithm picks out its most important sentences for the summary, key points and "why it matters" — it doesn't rewrite the story or invent anything. For papers, the authors' own abstract is used. Always check the original for details; it's one tap away.

在你的手机上完成：从原文中挑出最重要的句子作为摘要和要点，不改写、不编造；论文直接使用作者摘要。细节请以原文为准。

Why do some stories only show a short excerpt?为什么有些文章只有简短导语？

New stories first show the source's own excerpt, then get a full summary in the background after the article is read — the banner on the News tab shows how many are left. Open a story to process it right away. A few sites block apps or need JavaScript to show text; for those you'll see the excerpt and a note to read the original.

新文章先显示来源导语，后台读取原文后补全摘要；点开文章会立即处理。少数网站限制访问，只能显示导语。

Do I need an account or internet?需要注册或联网吗？

No account. You need a connection to fetch new stories; stories already loaded — and your saved ones — can be read offline.

How do I read in Chinese?如何切换中文？

Settings › Reading language › 中文 or 中英双语 (bilingual), or tap the translate button on any story. Translation uses Apple's on-device engine; the first time, iOS may ask to download the Chinese language pack. Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French and German are included with Pro.

设置 › 阅读语言 › 中文或双语；首次使用时系统会提示下载中文语言包。

How does the daily briefing and check-in work?每日简报和打卡怎么用？

Each day OpenWire picks about 10 must-reads from the last day and a half — top news, enterprise AI and a deep-dive. Read every story in the briefing to check in for the day and grow your streak. Turn on a daily reminder in Settings › Daily Check-in.

A source isn't loading — what can I do?某个来源加载不出来？

Pull down to refresh. Sources that failed last time show a ⚠︎ in Settings › Sources. If one keeps failing, try Settings › Sources › Clear cache & re-fetch, and email us — sites change their pages from time to time and we fix them in updates.

How do I add my own RSS source? (Pro)如何添加自定义来源？

Settings › Pro › My Sources › +. Paste a blog's homepage or feed address — OpenWire finds the RSS feed, shows its latest posts, and lets you pick which tab it appears in. Up to 30 sources, synced across your devices via iCloud.

How do keyword alerts work? (Pro)关键词提醒怎么用？

Settings › Pro › Keyword Alerts. Add up to 20 keywords (English or Chinese, e.g. "Claude", "MCP", "RAG"). Matching stories are listed on that screen, and you get a notification for new ones. While the app is open alerts arrive right away; in the background, iOS decides when to refresh (usually every few hours), so keep Background App Refresh on for OpenWire.

App 打开时即时提醒；后台刷新时机由 iOS 决定，请在系统设置中为开讯开启“后台 App 刷新”。

Where are my saved stories? (Pro)收藏在哪里？

Tap the bookmark on any story; find them in Settings › Library › Saved. Saved stories keep their full summary forever — even after they leave the feed — and sync via iCloud.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?免费版和 Pro 有什么区别？

Free includes all built-in sources, summaries, the daily briefing and check-in, English/Chinese/bilingual reading, the last 14 days of stories and both themes. Pro is a one-time $9.99 purchase — not a subscription: your own RSS sources, keyword alerts, saved stories kept forever, the full archive and five more reading languages. Family Sharing is supported.

How do I restore my purchase?如何恢复购买？

Sign in with the Apple Account you purchased with, then tap Settings › Pro › Restore Purchases, or Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Pro screen.

How do I request a refund?如何申请退款？

Visit reportaproblem.apple.com. Refunds are reviewed and handled by Apple.

How do I switch between dark and light?如何切换深色 / 浅色？

Settings › Theme: Amber Dark (with Serif White, Classic, Dark and Sepia reading themes in each story) or Signal (clean white).

Is my data private?我的数据安全吗？

Yes. No account, no ads, no tracking, and no servers — see the Privacy Policy.
